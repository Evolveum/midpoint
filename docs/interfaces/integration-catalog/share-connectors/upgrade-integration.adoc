= Upgrade existing integration
:experimental:
:page-description: This describes how to upgrade your custom integration method that you have previously created in the Integration catalog.
:page-keywords: integration catalog, integration, share connector, upload connector, share connectors, upload integration, midpoint integration

This describes how to upgrade your custom integration method that you have previously created in the xref:/midpoint/reference/interfaces/integration-catalog/[Integration catalog].


As integrations may become dated over time, you may want to update and publish a new version of an integration.

This process shares the same configuration options with the xref:/midpoint/reference/interfaces/integration-catalog/share-connectors/[uploading custom connectors] process.

. Make sure you have xref:/midpoint/reference/interfaces/integration-catalog/#features[sufficient privileges] to contribute to the Integration catalog. 
. xref:/midpoint/reference/interfaces/integration-catalog/share-connectors/#licenses[Search for the application] for which you want to publish a new integration version in the Integration catalog.
. Open it by clicking it and hover your mouse over the integration that you want to upgrade.

+
.Hover your mouse over an integration
image::../ic-application-detail.webp["Hover your mouse over an integration"]

. Click [.nowrap]#icon:edit[] btn:[Edit and upgrade]#.
. Update any information you need.
. At the bottom of the page:
** Click [.nowrap]#icon:arrow-up[] btn:[Save as new version]#,
** or click [.nowrap]#icon:save[] btn:[Save]# to override the integration with your changes.
